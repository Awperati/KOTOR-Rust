# EACH JOINT TAKES UP TO THREE ANGLES IN DEGREES: bend  out  twist
#   bend    curls the joint in. Knee to the chest, heel to the bottom, chin to the chest, elbow closed.
#   out     moves the limb away from the body, to the side. A minus brings it across the body.
#   twist   turns the limb about its own length, its front going outward. A minus turns it inward.
# Left and right work alike: to keep the body even, give both sides the same numbers.
#
#   JOINT                       WHAT IT IS         SAFE BEND    SAFE OUT    SAFE TWIST
#   lthigh_g  rthigh_g          hip                -30 to 140   -30 to 60   -40 to 40
#   lshin_g   rshin_g           knee                 0 to 150   do not use  do not use
#   lfoot_g   rfoot_g           ankle              -50 to 35    -20 to 20   -30 to 30
#   torso_g   torsoupr_g        lower, upper back  -30 to 50    -30 to 30   -40 to 40
#   necklwr_g neck_g  head_g    neck and head      -30 to 45    -30 to 30   -60 to 60
#   lcollar_g rcollar_g         shoulder blade     -20 to 20    -20 to 20   do not use
#   lbicep_g  rbicep_g          shoulder           -60 to 170   -40 to 120  -70 to 70
#   lforearm_g rforearm_g       elbow                0 to 145   do not use  -70 to 70
#   lhand_g   rhand_g           wrist              -60 to 60    -20 to 20   do not use
#   pelvis_g                    the hips           -30 to 30    -15 to 15   -30 to 30    It moves the legs, not the back.
# The game refuses only an angle past 180 either way. Inside that, anything goes: the safe ranges are where a body can bend.
#
